For those of you who’ve been living under a rock, I’d like to introduce you to sharing.

I’m not talking about the type of sharing my parents begged me to do as a kid, while I blatantly ignored their pleas and hid all my candy under my bed (as a parent, I finally understand the struggle).

Did you know there’s a type of sharing that can actually help get your family life super-organized, and you can still keep all your M&Ms for yourself (if you’re so inclined)?

If you’ll indulge me (as if you haven’t been indulging me enough already), I’d like to tell you a quick story.

The other day was one of those rare Fridays that I was out of work early enough to pick up my son from his after school daycare. As I was heading up the elevator to his classroom, I noticed a flyer next to the elevator buttons, immediately filling me with an unbridled joy and excitement that only parents would understand.

![A printed flyer in a plastic sleeve on a wooden wall: Bedok Children's Day Carnival 2026, Saturday 3 October, 2pm to 5pm, Siglap CC basketball court, with bouncy castles, a magic show and a $5 food and goodie bag](/blog/sharing-flyer-bedok-carnival.webp)

_any parent here will know the feeling you get when you see something like this_

An entire half day of kids’ entertainment, planned, choreographed, and arranged. And to top it off, a parent’s most favorite word in the whole entire world: free (or, well, close enough).

My hands fumbled clumsily into my pocket to grab my phone, almost dropping it down the closing doors of the elevator shaft. I hastily snapped a pic, my mind immediately freeing itself of one more half day of [family planning, organization, and entertainment](https://beanies.family/guides/overwhelmed-family-planning?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=sharing-with-beanies&utm_content=pillar-overwhelmed).

_What now, though?_

Heading down the hall to locate my son’s class, I could feel my crooked flyer photo already sinking into the depths of my never-ending camera roll, buried deeply in an infinite gallery of family photos and reminder snaps, destined to be forgotten in the heap of baby pictures, horribly unflattering selfies, and blurry pseudo-artistic nightscape photos we (and our kids, when they swipe our phones) now take in this modern age of ubiquitous phone-camera-ism.

So what did I do instead, that took me exactly 5 seconds more? I [shared that snap to my beanies app](https://beanies.family/help/features/share-to-beanies?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=sharing-with-beanies&utm_content=help-share). You see where this is going, right?

![Animated phone recording: the carnival flyer photo is shared from the gallery to beanies.family, magic beans read it, and a filled-in activity appears on the family calendar with the date, time, location and notes](/blog/sharing-flyer-share-loop.webp)

_literally took me 5 seconds. ok, with the extra time spent marveling at my newfound efficiency, maybe 10. what a beautiful thing._

Done, and dusted. Not only is the photo itself now saved together with the activity in my family calendar, but we also have the date, time, category, location and lots more. And what else? Every last (significant) item from the invite has been summarized to bullet points and saved in the notes section, so you don't have to sweat the details. And given it’s a celebration, a [helpful hint](https://beanies.family/blog/my-life-could-use-some-helpful-hints?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=sharing-with-beanies&utm_content=helpful-hints-post) will appear in my to-do list and daily briefing as a reminder when the time comes.

I know: You're welcome. _Again._

By the way, in case you were curious - yes, [it works with travel plans, too](https://beanies.family/blog/travel-plans-intro?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=sharing-with-beanies&utm_content=travel-plans-post):

![Animated phone recording: an airline confirmation email is shared to beanies.family and becomes a travel plan with the flights, times and notes filled in](/blog/sharing-travel-share-loop.webp)

_don’t complain to me the next time you forgot to purchase a child’s meal for your kid. beanies tried to tell you._

Share an image, a screenshot of your itinerary, a PDF document, or just copy and share an email or WhatsApp message, and boom! You have yourself a travel plan with every detail summarized and populated, down to your meal orders and how many pounds of luggage you can carry on the flight (as long as that info was buried somewhere in that forwarded document which - admit it - you didn’t even bother to read).

[Magic beans scan everything you send](https://beanies.family/help/security/how-beanies-ai-handles-your-photos?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=sharing-with-beanies&utm_content=help-magic-beans), down to the last detail of the terms and conditions, extracts everything that might be important or useful to you, and loads it straight into your calendar or travel plan as a beautiful, itemized plan and bulleted summary. And as per the legally documented and agreed terms of our [private and secure AI provider](https://beanies.family/blog/welcome-our-new-ai-overlords?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=sharing-with-beanies&utm_content=ai-provider), it immediately proceeds to forget everything you just sent as soon as it’s done.

If those aren't some helpful beans, I don't know what are.

![Animated cartoon: captain beanie, a smiling bean in a knitted hat and cape, flies out of a burning apartment window carrying a rescued baby beanie](/blog/captain-beanie-saves-baby.webp)

_seriously though, can somebody please get me a captain beanie plushie?_

Ok, so aside from an imaginary magical superhero that rescues little beanies from fires (that may be my next Netflix pitch idea), these are the most helpful beans I can think of. For now, anyway.

Take your family’s private and secure beanpod on [beanies.family](https://beanies.family/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=sharing-with-beanies&utm_content=closing-mention) to the next level with magic beans, and share (or don’t share) anything you'd like with the app. You don't even have to tell us what it is - we'll figure that out for you. We’ll even let you know if we got lost somewhere (just don't be a smart aleck and share some random 100-page PDF just to see if it works. It does. For the most part. You can trust me.)

I hope you find this useful, because I personally do. If you like it or think I should change anything, _[let me know on discord](https://beanies.family/discord?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=sharing-with-beanies&utm_content=discord-cta)!_

Enjoy those magic beans, my friends. And never stop sharing (the good kind, and the beanie kind).
