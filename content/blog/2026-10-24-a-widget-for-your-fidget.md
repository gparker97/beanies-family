Good morning, my beans. Yes, this is still me writing these. AI could never come up with this many random bean metaphors, of that I’m sure.

I’ve been especially busy this week with a special surprise project (which I’ll reveal soon), but I just wanted to say how wonderful it was to wake up a few days ago to the feedback below from one of our amazing early beanies (edited for length and clarity):

_“Hi, i love this app tbh and i created for my whole family. Now i could plan the days ahead without the need to text everyone one by one. They can check it in their own app. But i would love more if i can add a widget of huge calendar on my home screen or any widget will do. Other than that all is perfect! Tqsm again for this app.”_

_-early beanie Ummu_

It’s great hearing from my beans, and it’s even better when they ask for stuff that I can build.

I’m a huge widget-lover (don’t tell my wife), and I’ve had a beanies widget sitting idly on my to-do list for what seems like forever. I kept putting it off, as I couldn’t justify building a widget when so many other high-priority data and security issues were sitting on top, but Ummu’s feedback was the perfect excuse to get it off the bench and into beanies.family!

Great home screen widgets allow you to get information without having to lift a finger - at a glance, I can know whether it’ll rain tomorrow, the dollar-yen exchange rate, my number of unread emails, and the current time in Siberia. The best widgets give you a lot of options and configurability; the bad ones force you into one way of working and poor designs. So when it came time to build a [beanies.family](http://beanies.family/) widget, I knew we had to do it right.

So, without further ado, I give you: **_the [beanies.family](http://beanies.family/) widgets_**!

![An iPhone lock screen, an iPhone home screen and an Android home screen, each showing beanies.family widgets with the day’s activities, to-dos and dinner.](/blog/a-widget-for-your-fidget-widgets.webp)

_i love fidgeting with widgets_

**I love widgets too! What can I see?**

I feel like lots of widgets shortchange you on available information, and many don’t give you enough space to truly get everything you want to see. I designed our beanies widgets in 3 sizes, all of which allow you to choose the information you want - activities, travel plans, to-dos, lists, birthdays, hints and more. I have all 3 of them on my phone, but then again, I’m probably not your typical [beanies.family](http://beanies.family/) user. You can choose the size that works best for you, and that’s what makes this country great (I’m not actually sure which country you’re in, but I’m sure it’s great).

There’s even a lock screen widget for iPhones, which I didn’t even realize was something you could do. Seeing the beanies lock widget on my iPhone though, I love having my next activity staring me in the face when I pick up my phone (provided that’s not a privacy concern - that’s up to you).

For those who prefer larger text, the text size is also configurable. Personally, I keep text on my phone screen almost impossibly small, as I’m in a period of active old man eyesight age denial, and I strongly feel that having minuscule phone screen text will somehow reverse my eyeballs’ inevitable death march towards full-blown bi-focal-land. I am squinting as I write this.

In any case, I love our [beanies.family](http://beanies.family/) widget (the parts that I can make out, anyway), and I hope you do too!

**Give it a try!**

I’m curious to hear what all of you think (especially the early beanie who asked for this in the first place)! So update your app, add the widget to your home screen (it’s already installed if you have the latest app), and let me know your thoughts!

If you’re not sure how to install a widget, it’s almost always by a long-tap on an empty area of your phone’s home screen, then choose beanies.family from the list of available widgets.

As per my usual feedback response pattern, it was less than a week from feedback to released feature! I’m excited that we could get this out there quickly, and I hope this is useful for everyone!

Enjoy your new widgets, my beans. Speak soon.

-greg
