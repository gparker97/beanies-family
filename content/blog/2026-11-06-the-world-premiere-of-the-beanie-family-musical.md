**The beanie family musical has been released!**

<video controls playsinline preload="none" poster="https://beanies.family/media/musical/v2/poster-1280.webp" aria-label="the beanie family, a real life musical"><source src="https://beanies.family/media/musical/v2/film-1080.mp4" type="video/mp4" /></video>

_i spent all that time (and money) on this?_

You can also [watch it on YouTube](https://youtu.be/Ye2d70NJTUk).

First came the melody. Then, the lyrics. Then the notation, instrumentation, background instrumental, the synthetic AI voices produced in SynthV (NOT cheap, by the way), and the overall music production.

Then came the animation part: storyboarding with nano-banana, animation testing with omni, kling, flux, and several other models (by the way - also NOT cheap), and then (literally) hundreds (maybe thousands) of rounds of tweaking, re-writes, re-drawing, and re-thinking every major decision I’ve ever made in my life that led me up to this point.

But, after what has been one of the craziest stretches of my experience to this point building beanies.family, I’m proud to announce that I’m roughly S$967 poorer. But also, the world premiere of **THE BEANIE FAMILY - a real life musical** is here!

I can’t say exactly why I did this, other than I woke up one day with a dumb tune stuck in my head, attached some lyrics to it, and realized it might be a fun video. Being somewhat of an OCD perfectionist, the several weeks that followed were more than an ordeal. Maybe one day I’ll write a post about actually putting this thing together. For now, I think I just need to get some sleep.

What do you think? Be brutal, I can take it. Actually, what am I talking about? No I can’t (and my _claude-bot_ will not tolerate people being mean to me). I’ve probably poured way too much of my time and energy into this to absorb negative criticism gracefully. So, tell me what you think, but be nice 🙂

Enjoy, my beanies. I hope you like it.

-greg (assistant creative director, composer, lyricist, and animator, [beanies.family](http://beanies.family/). Right after my wife.)
