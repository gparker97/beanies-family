**So, how was this actually made? (If you care)**

Well, I woke up one morning (technically, it was probably still nighttime), as people sometimes do, with a dumb tune stuck in my head. I attached some lyrics to it, and thought it might be a fun video. I (naively, as we would soon discover) thought AI would probably do most of the work. After all, it’s the age of AI, right?! The several weeks that followed were a bit of an ordeal, to put it mildly.

**Going from my head to actual paper**

After the melody came the lyrics and notation, using an amazing software called MuseScore, which I only just discovered for this project. In the past, I’d been a user of Finale, and going WAY back, my go-to for scoring was Deluxe Music on my Amiga 3000 - man, that takes me back. This was literally the only way for me to get this dumb song out of my head, so it could finally start annoying somebody else.

![The MuseScore score for the beanie family musical at 140 BPM: four voice staves with the lyrics, plus electric piano, electric bass and drum kit](/blog/the-beanie-family-musical-musescore.webp 'wide')

_actually listening to this with midi instruments assigned to each staff was hilarious_

**How could we get it to sound like a musical, though?**

The instrumental backing track is a Suno cover based on the MuseScore notation, which blew me away. I had no idea there existed AI models that could generate backing tracks that sounded this amazing, simply based off of a few chords and basslines, but track after track came out (more or less) matching the general shape of my song and prompts. It took a few days (and _lots_ of experimentation), but after some cutting and stretching, we finally got something we could use.

![The Suno song editor with the style prompt for an animated-feature soundtrack in C major at a strict 140 BPM, and the generated track's waveform](/blog/the-beanie-family-musical-suno.webp 'wide')

_the biggest struggle here was that you can’t easily tweak what suno spits out, aside from cutting it or stretching it out. so this was a challenge, to say the least._

**We’ve got instruments. What about singing?**

After that, of course, we needed actual words. I used Synthesizer V Studio 2 Pro to produce the vocals and do some overall music production (NOT cheap, by the way, and in case anybody was wondering, no, none of this is sponsored).

![Synthesizer V Studio 2 Pro with separate vocal tracks for mom, dad, girl and boy, and the piano roll showing the phonemes for "oh, what's for dinner"](/blog/the-beanie-family-musical-synthesizer-v.webp 'wide')

_another thing that blew me away - synthetic voice technology has really come a long way._

**So we defied the odds and somehow created a song... _now_ what?**

After the song, we needed actual video: Next was creating the character designs, storyboarding with Nano Banana, animation testing with several different models (by the way - also NOT cheap), and then hundreds (actually, maybe thousands) of rounds of tweaking, re-writes, re-drawing, and re-thinking every major decision I’ve ever made in my life that led me up to this point.

![A grid of 48 tiny animation frames from the kitchen scene, two versions of the film side by side, each frame stamped with its version and timecode](/blog/the-beanie-family-musical-animation-strips.webp 'wide')

_reviewing animation strips is the kind of thing that will make you want to jump off an animated bridge holding a “goodbye” sign_

So, after what has been one of the weirder stretches of my life to this point building [beanies.family](https://beanies.family/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=the-world-premiere-of-the-beanie-family-musical&utm_content=body), I’m proud to announce that I’m roughly $967 poorer. But I’m also much _more_ pleased to announce the world premiere of [**THE BEANIE FAMILY - a real life musical**](https://beanies.family/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=the-world-premiere-of-the-beanie-family-musical&utm_content=film-title#musical)!

There’s a lot more to say - maybe one day I’ll write a post with even more (excruciating) detail about the process of _actually_ putting this thing together. But for now, I think I just need to get some sleep.

**… Well?**

Enjoy, my beanies, and I hope you like it.
