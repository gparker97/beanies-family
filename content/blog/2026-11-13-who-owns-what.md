I got a bit ahead of myself the other day and made a post on the r/FairPlayLife sub on Reddit that was perhaps a bit premature. I thought it was no big deal, but a few people ended up getting mad, and one person actually deleted his beanpod. Oops.

I didn’t realize what a passionate bunch _Fair Play Lifers_ are. Which, by the way, includes me now.

Let me back up.

## The book that inspired a trend

Around when I started building [beanies.family](https://beanies.family/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=who-owns-what&utm_content=homepage-mention), I read (and quickly finished) the book _Fair Play_ by Eve Rodsky, and went on to talk about it in one of my [organization-focused family guides](https://beanies.family/guides/overwhelmed-family-planning?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=who-owns-what&utm_content=pillar-overwhelmed-family-planning#family-overwhelm-is-a-compounding-problem). The concepts in the book guided my thoughts on family organization and planning, and have helped shape how I’ve built (and am building) beanies.family. In short, it’s a great book, and an easy read on family planning and organization, and specifically lays out practical ways to split family-management related responsibilities between partners.

I was browsing reddit the other day (which, I’ll admit, is probably a bad habit) and ran across the r/FairPlayLife subreddit. I was surprised to see that a Fair Play-inspired sub existed, and was immediately inspired to post. I was keen to talk about my thoughts on the book, but I also realized that families who are passionate about organizing their family life and responsibility split are likely to be naturally interested in family organization platforms. So, off I went.

I talked about how “Fair Play” inspired several features in the app, and how it’s inspired me to build a “Fair Play” type system into [beanies.family](https://beanies.family/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=who-owns-what&utm_content=homepage-mention-2). Unfortunately, some people took that as beanies.family having those features already.

## So - let me apologize

Firstly, I’m sorry - I got ahead of myself. I was inspired at the time, and I honestly didn’t think that many people would read my post.

But, **secondly**, let me very proudly announce that I’m not ahead of myself anymore. Yes, we are now a _fully-formed, battle-tested, 100% compliant_ (whatever that means) family responsibility platform - let me proudly introduce: **_<a href="https://app.beanies.family/who-owns-what" target="_blank" rel="noopener" data-cta="CTA: Create Pod" data-cta-loc="blog-who-owns-what">who owns what</a>_**!

As a type-A, organization-oriented (some - like my wife - might say “obsessed”) person, I **_love_** the concept of a family breakdown of responsibilities that is shared, clear, and explicit. When division of labor is messy, and responsibilities are not clear, that’s when systems of ownership break down, and hapless kids get left waiting at soccer practice in the rain (hypothetically speaking, of course).

When the breakdown of responsibilities is clean, clear, and explicit, that’s when parents are on the same page.

![A collage of the who owns what page in beanies.family: the overview ring showing 72 of 75 cards dealt, the breakdown by category, one parent's cards, the family check-in card and the deck on a phone.](/blog/who-owns-what-collage.webp 'wide')

_our family responsibilities live here now. and forever (or at least until the kids are old enough to drive themselves to practice)._

There are roughly 80 different cards to own, all broken down into nine categories. That may seem like a lot, but the truth is that’s simply the reality of running a household. As Eve Rodsky put it in _Fair Play_, there’s a ton of sh*t to do, and there’s not really any way around that for a household with any amount of children.

**The question is - how do we actually apply these concepts of family management and organization to our daily routine?**

I love when I ask myself questions, because I always know the answer.

In addition to hosting your family responsibilities, I wanted [beanies.family](https://beanies.family/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=who-owns-what&utm_content=homepage-mention-3) to be **_more than just a system of record_**. Knowing who owns what is great, but how could we actually materialize that ownership in your daily life?

So in beanies.family, the _who owns what_ section gives you the ability to clearly define responsibilities, and on top of that, it **actively integrates** into your family’s daily life.

- Any card can carry a **_family reminder_**: switch it on and the holder gets a recurring to-do, which shows up in the to-do section of the app
- Cards can be **_split by child, or into any custom parts_** you can think of
- **_Custom cards_** can be created to handle your specific family life - no need to rely on just the ones we added to the default set
- **_Card holders become the default owner_** where it makes sense - whoever holds cooking dinner is the default cook in the meal planner, and new grocery lists go to whoever holds grocery shopping
- **_Family check-in dates_** are scheduled to review changed and unassigned cards
- **_All of this is configurable_** in the _who owns what_ section
- Probably **_other stuff too_** that I’m not thinking of - just go explore for yourself!

![Dealing cards in who owns what on a phone: swiping through the pile, keeping a card and choosing who owns it, then splitting one card by child.](/blog/who-owns-what-deal-cards.webp)

_if only things were this simple at the office_

## But wait - what about my ‘unicorn space’?

One of my favorite card categories is ‘just for me’ - this is what you hold when you’ve done the hard work, you know who owns what, and you set aside time for yourself. This can be literally anything - as long as it helps you feel more relaxed and calm, which is what this whole thing is all about.

For me, I hold time to myself for things like exercise, runs (2 or 3 times a week, or whenever I feel like I can’t take the stress anymore), and sports (if you want to see an old man fumble around a softball field, just follow me on any given Sunday). These are my ‘just for me’ affordances, and they’re part of what gets us back to feeling like ourselves.

Getting _who owns what_ into a workable feature for [beanies.family](https://beanies.family/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=who-owns-what&utm_content=homepage-mention-4) took more rounds of mockups, planning, back and forth coding sessions, and bug and enhancement reviews than I probably care to mention, but this is a feature that I really wanted to get right. I’m keen to hear your thoughts - especially those people on r/FairPlayLife that were so desperately searching for a feature that wasn’t quite cooked yet - apologies (again) for that!

So even if you haven’t read the book, give _<a href="https://app.beanies.family/who-owns-what" target="_blank" rel="noopener" data-cta="CTA: Create Pod" data-cta-loc="blog-who-owns-what">who owns what</a>_ a try for your household, and [let me know on discord](https://beanies.family/discord?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=who-owns-what&utm_content=discord) how it works!

When _who owns what_ becomes not just a question, but a statement, that’s when your life starts to settle into order. Give it a try, and get those responsibilities down pat.
