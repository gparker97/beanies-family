**📢 Announcement: Another family organization app bites the dust (not that we’re gloating or anything) 📢**

Ollie, a family AI assistant that allowed you to text it like a person, [shut down on November 6](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/30/ollie-a-privacy-focused-ai-assistant-is-shutting-down/). The announcement was only released a couple weeks ago, so families literally got _one week_ of notice before their data was _gonzo_. Not very thoughtful, although to be fair, these situations can be chaotic.

Maple, which [I wrote about back in August](https://beanies.family/blog/maple-alternative?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=ollie-alternative&utm_content=maple-post), turns off the lights for good on December 31.

Ollie and Maple were two extremely well-funded family apps (Ollie at about $7.5M, and Maple at $5M in seed funding), and both are gone in the blink of an eye (well - in cosmic terms). If you used either one, I'm sorry for your loss - letting go of the thing that holds your family together (in any form) is a real pain. Here’s the good news, though: it doesn’t have to happen again.

## Why beanies.family will always be there

[beanies.family](https://beanies.family/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=ollie-alternative&utm_content=homepage-intro) is open source, local-first, and free if you host it yourself. I can’t read your data because I can’t even access it. Even if you use the hosted version on beanies.family (which is not free, although [it shouldn’t break the bank](https://beanies.family/pricing?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=ollie-alternative&utm_content=pricing)) your data is _still_ always safe, since it sits on your own storage, not mine. That's the idea behind [local-first](https://beanies.family/guides/local-first-family-finance-planning-tools?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=ollie-alternative&utm_content=pillar-local-first).

beanies.family keeps all the things Maple and Ollie did, like your family calendar, to-dos, lists, meal plans and recipes in one place, plus the family finances if you want them. Everything Maple did, it does. Since I last wrote about Maple it's also picked up a meal planner, a choreboard where the kids earn stars toward rewards, home-screen widgets for iPhone and Android, the weather right next to your family's plans, and lots more stuff that’s not popping into my head at this exact moment.

What’s more? beanies.family is genuinely a fun app to use. Even more so if you like cute beans.

## The email thing, minus the snooping

Based on what I could gather from their homepage, one of the best parts of Ollie was turning school emails into calendar events. This is great, but to me, the catch was how it did it - it read your inbox. All your emails, about anything, from anyone. I don’t know about you, but some of that stuff I may have wanted to keep to myself.

beanies.family can do the same thing, we just don’t snoop around your inbox or other apps. When that swimming competition email arrives, simply [share it to beanies from your phone](https://beanies.family/blog/sharing-with-beanies?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=ollie-alternative&utm_content=sharing-post) (or just paste the text in), and magic beans turns it into an activity or a to-do. It reads only that email, and only one time. We don’t grant an all-you-can-eat buffet key to your inbox.
