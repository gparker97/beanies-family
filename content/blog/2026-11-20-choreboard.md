What makes a great family choreboard? Well, in my view it’s any choreboard that _actually_ motivates your kids to do their chores!

There’s honestly not much more to say, but there’s _a lot_ to show. But if I stop here my _claude-bot_ will probably scold me for not meeting the requisite word count for my weekly blogs.

In all seriousness, raising your kids to be better, more responsible human beings is one of the core challenges of parenthood. [A strong, reliable chores routine](https://beanies.family/guides/family-organization?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=choreboard&utm_content=pillar-family-organization) is a great first step towards that goal. But how do we actually engage the kids to get it done?

Lots of apps have tried. They’ve used special lists, animations, points, and more. But if it’s not built in the right way, it just doesn’t engage the kids the way it should. Even beanies is not immune to this - I initially used lists as a substitute for a true choreboard. It worked, but it wasn’t ideal - chores are their own thing. A properly done choreboard requires organization, structure, and that special something which is hard to put your finger on (maybe it’s a bean?).

Given that beanies.family already has the [beanie wall and digital calendar](https://beanies.family/blog/beanie-wall-for-your-whole-family?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=choreboard&utm_content=beanie-wall-post), it’s a perfect place to add a (proper) choreboard. It’s already situated in your living room, or a place your family (and kids) frequent, and your family looks at it every day. In my house, the beanie wall sits right outside the kitchen, and the kids always pass it on their way out the door.

But anyway, as I mentioned earlier, talk is cheap. What can the beanie choreboard actually do?!

_if little beanies actually parachuted into my living room every time i finished my chores when i was a kid, i would probably have done them more often_

There’s now so much more to the beanie wall choreboard than just a list of chores. The new beanies.family choreboard allows you to organize chores by time of day (morning, afternoon, or evening), allows you to set the hours you choose for each category, create chores that can be done anytime, lets your kids build stars for every chore they complete, and more. You can even customize the number of stars for each chore, and create weekly and special rewards your kids can earn. And yes - before you even ask - you can award bonus stars when they’re good, and take stars away when they’re bad (honestly - how else will those naughty buggers learn?).

And if your kids are at all interested in earning their rewards, they’ll actually improve their counting and math skills at the same time. “See Joey, only 12 more stars until your special ice cream reward - and don’t forget, reading for 30 minutes is worth 3 stars!”

On my side, the weekly reward is their allowance, and special rewards are things like ice cream and indoor playgrounds (which they beg for every weekend). There’s a full star report and dashboard where you can actually go back to check and see if they’ve earned their keep - all the data is there!
