Hello to all my traveling beans!

I’ve been sitting here, [planning travel](https://beanies.family/blog/travel-plans-intro?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=flight-change-alerts&utm_content=travel-plans-post), still using two apps at once. And I asked myself, _why_?

Since [beanies.family](https://beanies.family/?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=flight-change-alerts&utm_content=homepage-flight-alerts) takes in my travel details with [a simple share](https://beanies.family/blog/sharing-with-beanies?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=flight-change-alerts&utm_content=sharing-post), displays all the details in an easy to read format, allows me to easily make any changes or updates either manually or with AI, keeps lists of things I might want to do, and [shares everything seamlessly with my family](https://beanies.family/guides/family-organization?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=flight-change-alerts&utm_content=family-organization-guide), why is my other travel app still hanging around?

[_Real-time travel alerts._](https://beanies.family/help/features/live-flight-checks?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=flight-change-alerts&utm_content=help-live-flight-checks)

Well, now they’re here, and you (or, well, me) can finally throw away that second travel app.

![A flight card in a beanies.family travel plan, marked Changed: SQ318 from Singapore to London now leaves at 6:30pm from Terminal 3, with the old 5pm time struck through, a note saying the plan has been updated, and buttons to put it back or dismiss it.](/blog/flight-change-alert-card.webp)

_the feature we’ve all been waiting for. well, i have, at least._

## Here’s what travel alerts can do

- Check your flight number and date against the airline
- Tell you when your flight’s time, date or terminal changes, or it’s cancelled, starting from 3 days before your planned departure date
- Keep checking whether your flight is still on schedule, and on the phone apps tell you _even when beanies is closed_
- Let you know if the terminal changed

## Here’s how much privacy you give up to use this feature

- Only the flight number and date leave your device, with no name, family or booking attached

beanies checks that flight number and date against an industry standard flight status database API - therefore while we are running checks against airlines and flight numbers, _nothing_ about your travel plans can be linked back to you. On the phone apps, a push address is also held until after the flight, so the alert can reach you.

Once the data comes back, anything you need to know about is alerted to your family without your personal data ever being shared.

So it’s a helpful and fully private check of your travel plans to ensure you (or your family) are not caught at the wrong time, date, or terminal.

I’ve already started using this for my own travel plans, and, while it’s not saved me yet (I’m quite obsessive about checking flight status when I have trips coming up), I’m feeling much more confident that when that day comes in the future, I’ll know far in advance if I need to start changing my plans (which always makes me cringe, bro).
