what's changed & why

A

Hero: "privacy, guaranteed." is pulled out of the run-on sentence into its own beat, and rendered in the hero's existing orange→terracotta gradient — the same treatment as "counts." in the headline. It reuses a brand signature rather than inventing a new highlight, and pairs the claim with its proof ("never leaves your hands") so "guaranteed" reads as architecture, not marketing. "Hands" — not "device" — stays literally true even with Google Drive sync on: the .beanpod is ciphertext locked to a key only you hold, so it's always in your hands, never Google's. The green 🔒 local-first chip echoes the existing floating AES-256 encrypted card already in the live hero.

B

Onboarding: the reassurance line mirrors the hero — "your data stays with you. privacy, guaranteed." The trigger is a "how?" pill (word + ? badge), a much bigger tap target than a lone 16px icon and it states intent. It opens the existing InfoHintBadge popover (used in 10+ places) in items mode — three scannable proofs — plus one small new capability: an optional "learn how it works →" link at the foot.

C

Link destination: /help/security/zero-knowledge-architecture — the real help article titled "Zero-Knowledge Architecture" ("We can't see your data. Period."). A far better home than the legal /privacy page.
