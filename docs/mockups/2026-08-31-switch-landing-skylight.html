the part where i'm honest

skylight's hardware is genuinely nice

The screen is lovely, the mounting is clean, and there's real joy in a calendar the whole family can actually see. Skylight got families to look up from their phones and at the same wall, and credit where it's due.

My quibble is what it costs to get there: a second tablet for a house that probably owns three, and a subscription on top. And the part nobody mentions: everything on that lovely screen lives on their cloud, not yours.

"having a comprehensive set of awesome family organization and finance planning related features while also being fully private are two things that are no longer mutually exclusive." - me, in an actual interview, sounding smarter than usual

So keep the wall calendar idea. It's a good one. Just point it at your own data, on your own screen, for free.

have your cake, and eat it too

private doesn't mean less anymore

the same three cards, borrowed straight from the homepage security section.

🙈 encrypted means encrypted

Most apps encrypt your data on their servers, with their keys, which is a security feature. beanies encrypts it before it leaves your device, with your key, which is a privacy feature. I can't read it, sell it, or lose it, because I never had it in the first place.

📂 one file, yours

Everything lives in a single encrypted .beanpod file on your Google Drive or device. Lose your password? I can't help you. That's kinda the whole point.

🤷 nothing to steal

Even if a hacker wanted to poke around our dark corner of the internet, there's nothing there except code and app configuration. Good luck finding anything to leak.

fair questions

things skylight folks actually ask

do i need to buy anything?

No. Any tablet with a browser works: iPad, Android, the one in the drawer with the cracked corner. A $20 wall mount if you're feeling fancy. That's the whole shopping list.

okay but what if YOU shut down?

No matter what happens to beanies.family in the future, your data is always yours. It belongs to you, and it's sitting on your own device or storage. Which is kind of the whole point.

is it hard to switch?

It's just a few minutes to set up. You can go through the onboarding, which makes importing your data dead simple. Our wizard walks you through every step.

why is it free?

Right now, I cover the costs myself; someday there'll be a fair price for families who want more, and I promise it won't burn a hole in your wallet. What there won't be, ever: ads. Or popups. Or anybody reading your family's data besides you, and your family. Which is the way it should be for your precious beans.
