The tick never changes. It is the biggest target on the row and the one thing a five year old uses, so nothing new goes near it. The row controls appear only while the wall is unlocked, which is already behind the PIN.

Rename: tap the words (not the tick) and the row becomes a box in place, shown here on "Towel". Remove: the trailing ✕, which deletes straight away and offers Undo in a toast rather than stopping the parent with a confirm dialog. The wall already has showToast and has never had a modal, so undo suits the surface better than a red confirm.

Why a spine rather than a flat list: a trip really is a sequence, so the rail is carrying information rather than decorating. It also makes "four flights" countable from across the room, which is the thing that failed. The date chip groups by day so the drawer answers "what happens Saturday" without reading every band.

Stays read as spans, not moments. A hotel node is a dashed rounded bracket rather than a disc, because a stay covers the nights between two flights. The band underneath already says check in and check out, so nothing new had to be invented to show it.

Kept as-is: the countdown hero, the booked count, the gold "needs booking" pill and SegmentWhenBand itself, so the wall and the trip page still agree about when a flight leaves. The small trip card on the wall keeps its two-leg cap; that one is doing its job.
