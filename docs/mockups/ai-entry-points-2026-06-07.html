Let beanies read it — AI entry points

Three ways to weave the new "read my document" capability into the paths families already know — the add buttons, the FAB sheet, and the create modals. One shared visual signal: a Heritage Orange → Terracotta shimmer and a single ✨. No purple, no robots. The magic looks like the beanies doing something delightful, never a bolted-on "AI" brand.

The agent is the beanies AI is something beanies do for you — "let beanies read it." The capability is reading-your-document; the beanies are who does it.
A peer, never a takeover It sits beside manual entry everywhere you create — discoverable, inviting, but never louder than the rest of the Nook.
What we deliberately avoid No purple gradients. No circuit/robot/brain icons. No "AI ✨ Powered" badge. The shimmer and one sparkle carry all the "magic."

The shared signal — and the inconsistency it fixes

Today the two entry points disagree: a bare camera circle on activities, a teal outlined pill on travel. One signal unifies them and adds a value promise.

Best for: making AI a genuine equal path rather than an "extra." Strongest framing of value, but it adds one step to every manual create — so it's best gated to entities where documents are common (activities, trips), with a "always type it in" preference for power users. Most engineering + most behavioural change.

Across all three, the same primitives do the work: a shared <MagicShimmer> surface (the orange→terracotta sheen + sparkle), one unified verb — "Read a document" with context variants ("Read the invite" / "Read my itinerary") — and the existing consent → file-picker → review flow underneath, untouched. The standalone page buttons (the camera circle, the teal pill) collapse into the same shimmer pill so the language is one thing everywhere.

Other surfaces worth a follow-up (not mocked here): a first-run "did you know beanies can read your invites?" spotlight in the notifications drawer; an empty-state nudge on a blank calendar/trip list; and a long-press on the FAB jumping straight to the camera. These layer on top of whichever strategy we pick.
