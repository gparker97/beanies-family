Why it looks like this

  • The bullets are beans. That is the one playful touch; everything else stays quiet.
  • Each bullet carries its role (what / how it helps / where) in small italic, so readers learn to jump straight to "where to find it".
  • The app path is drawn as a chip, because it's the line people act on.
  • The colour comes from the issue's tint, so the box belongs to that issue instead of looking like a generic callout.

How it's built

  • Frontmatter beanieBullets:, a 3-item tuple in the Zod schema: the build fails on 2 or 4.
  • The layout renders the box, not the markdown, so the drop cap on the first paragraph still lands on the story.
  • No field, no box: story posts (aloe vera, Japan) stay as they are.
  • Plain <ul> HTML near the top of the page, which AI crawlers can quote directly.
