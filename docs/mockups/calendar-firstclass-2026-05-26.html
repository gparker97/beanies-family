Your call · how trips share the stage

Three ways to demote the vacation bar

All three free the fold for the calendar — they differ in how visible trips stay. Pick one (or mix: B's in-grid band is compatible with A's ribbon, which is what the Month/Week mockups show).

A slim, collapsible strip of trip pills inside the command bar. Glanceable countdowns stay one look away; ~40px tall vs ~180px today.

My recommendation: A + B together

Use the in-grid band (B) as the always-on truth — trips appear on their real dates in every view — and add the collapsible ribbon (A) for at-a-glance countdowns to trips that are still weeks away. That's exactly what the Desktop Month / Week mockups show. The rich vacation card (progress bar, "3 nights unaccommodated") then lives on tap-through to the trip's own detail, not as permanent page chrome. C is the safest if you want minimal change, but it hides the trips you most want families to anticipate.
