Calendar reconnect surfaces

P1 mockup — the user-facing signal + one-tap recovery that turns the silent calendar disconnect into something you can see and fix. Mirrors the Drive GoogleReconnectToast pattern, restyled to the CIG (Heritage Orange, squircles, soft shadows).

Fonts approximate Outfit / Inter. Every colour, radius and shadow is a CIG token.

1 · The moment it stops syncing — App-level reconnect toast (mobile PWA)

Fires once per transition to needs_reconnect, not on every failed poll. Dismissible. Deduped — never stacks.

  • Idle: the signal that was missing — you now see that pushes stopped, without opening Settings.

Decision to confirm — toast colour

Drive's live reconnect toast is amber. The CIG designates Heritage Orange as the alert colour (amber isn't a brand colour). This mockup uses Heritage Orange. Recommended: build one shared reconnect-toast component in Heritage Orange and migrate Drive's amber toast onto it too — that gets both consistency (your explicit ask) and CIG-compliance in one move.
