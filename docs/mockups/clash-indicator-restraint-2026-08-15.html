Two states — the distinction has to survive losing the pill

Active — solid, full opacity
Acknowledged — 40% (was 70%)
old 70% — too close to active

Today the states are told apart by shape (pill vs mark). Once both are marks, opacity carries it alone — and 70% sits too close to solid to read at a glance. Dropping acknowledged to 40% keeps “I’ve handled this” legible without adding weight back.

Where the calendar name goes

Nowhere new — the drawer already says it in full, with the actions attached: “Overlaps your gregsophia@gmail.com calendar” · This is OK · Reschedule…. The grid signals; the drawer explains. On desktop the mark also keeps its native tooltip, and role="img" + aria-label="May clash with …" are unchanged, so screen readers still get the full sentence.
