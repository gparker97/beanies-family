The “?” popover (InfoHintBadge)

Why is there a record of past changes at all?

It is how two devices work out what each other has changed, so their edits can be brought together without anything being lost. It is not a backup, and nothing in your family's data depends on it.

Once every device is up to date there is nothing left for that record to reconcile, so it can be cleared. Your beans, and every date, note and photo, are untouched.

On an older phone or tablet the record can grow big enough to stop the file opening at all. Compacting is what fixes that.

Rendered by the existing InfoHintBadge, the same “?” badge used on Reminders, Todos and the Nook.
