Proposed — light

If your family file will not open

Over years of use, a family file collects a record of every change ever made. On an older phone or tablet that record can grow big enough to stop the file opening at all. beanies can rebuild it without that history. Your beans are not changed — only the record of how they got there.

Stage 4 · the “due” note appears here when the file is actually large

Only use this if beanies is slow to open, or will not open, on one of your devices. It is not routine tidying, it affects everyone in your family, and it cannot be undone.

beanies saves a copy of the current file first, next to it, so you can go back.
