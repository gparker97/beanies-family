Other modals in this flow

  • "Sign in another device" (the magic-link QR sheet you open from the profile menu) gets the same orange title band and icon square.
  • The PIN step-up used elsewhere (removing a member, transferring ownership) also gets the title band. Its behaviour is unchanged: if biometric is set up it still asks, but it skips the "which method?" chooser and goes straight to biometric, falling back to the PIN pad.
