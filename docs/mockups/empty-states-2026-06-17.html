The mascot kit (what changed)

Body: a real bean/egg silhouette (path, not a flat ellipse) in Heritage Orange / Terracotta / Deep Slate, with a soft top-left sheen. Hat: the signature — a knitted Sky-Silk cap with vertical ribs, a folded brim, and a pom-pom; every beanie wears one. Face: big round eyes with twin white sparkle highlights, blush cheeks, an open happy smile. Limbs: two little feet, and a stubby arm when the scene needs one (hugging the clipboard, holding the magnifier). The hands-held dashboard keeps the golden rule — beanies never separated. New lists scene: a beanie hugging a fresh checklist (two ticked, one dashed invite line) — an empty screen as an invitation to start.
