the first deal desktop · dark

from zero to a dealt deck in about five minutes

the empty state a brand-new family sees, in dark mode with the real shell. pick the cards that apply, hide the ones that don't, then hand them out.

let's deal the deck ✨

68 cards cover most of what keeps a home running: dinner, school forms, the bins, the big holidays. pick the ones that are yours, then hand them out. nobody gets stuck with everything.

  1. pick your cardshide any that aren't your family. add your own.
  2. deal them outtap a card, tap a bean. split the kids' cards by child.
  3. let beanies do the restcooks, list owners and gift hints fill themselves in.
you can re-deal any card, any time

about this mockup. Notion #109, family responsibilities, not built yet. sample family: greg, Sofia, Mia, Leo and Biscuit the dog. the shell (sidebar, header, pills, tiles, sheet, mobile chrome) mirrors the shipped app at v0.21.6.

assumptions for the open questions. the page sits in the treehouse between meal planner and the pod under the working name "who owns what" (short enough not to wrap in the sidebar). "what done looks like" is editable per family, one line, in the sheet. the deal check counts cards, with no scoring, and only speaks up for cards with nobody or a clearly lopsided deal. card radius is 16px to match list tiles, nested inside 24px surfaces. card names and done-lines are original; per-card illustrations would come from the nano-banana prompt list in the plan.
