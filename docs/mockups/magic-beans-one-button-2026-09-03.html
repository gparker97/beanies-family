#84 — one magic beans button

APPROVED: direction B. The card's gradient, badge and copy are the shipped component; only the affordance changes.

The question the design has to get right. The three chips today ask “what IS this?” — invite, travel booking, recipe — which is exactly the question the AI should be answering, and the one a user can get wrong. A source picker asks “where is it?” — camera, file, clipboard. That is mechanical, unavoidable, and not the same question. Every direction below keeps the second and removes the first.

Constraint carried from the codebase: on native, the mixed image+PDF accept routes to the documents picker with no camera entry — which is why AiDocumentPicker exists. A single button must not lose the camera.

Approved 2026-09-03: direction B. It is the only one where no “what kind of thing is this?” decision happens at any point — you either paste or tap a source — and it keeps the explicit camera button that AiDocumentPicker exists to preserve on native. The two rejected directions are kept at the foot as a record of the trade, not as alternatives.
