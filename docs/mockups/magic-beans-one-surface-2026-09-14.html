Magic beans: one surface, every door

Direction B of the 3 September mockup was approved and shipped as MagicBeansSheet.vue — but only behind the quick-add FAB. Four other doors still open a bare file picker wired to a pinned extractor. This proposes rolling the one surface out to all of them, and adds the one thing that makes full inference safe to lean on: the type question moves from before the read to after it.

1Where the six doors stand today

Two doors already infer and route anywhere. Four pin the answer before beanies has looked at anything. Activities and travel accept no text and no link at all, so a forwarded WhatsApp invite or an emailed itinerary has no way in on the page that owns it. The recipe doors do take a link, but only a link, and only a valid one.

2Why this is not just tidiness

The two spines are not cosmetically different, they are behaviourally different. useSharedDocumentIngest validates, budgets, locks and logs; the three per-page wedges re-implement a thinner version of each. Collapsing onto the one surface closes these as a side effect rather than as six separate tickets.

  • Two capture locks that do not see each other. isIngesting is module-global but the page wedges never consult it, so a page capture can run concurrently with a share or FAB ingest and bill two extractions at once.
  • Size and type checks exist on two doors of six. The 25 MB cap and the byte-sniff live in the ingest only. On the page wedges an oversized or mislabelled file goes straight into canvas compression and surfaces as photos.invalidType, which names the wrong problem.
  • Activities and travel never reach the per-family rate limit. Neither wedge passes familyId, so those two doors are held back only by the IP limit.
  • A refused reader is billed first. With aiTravelExtract off, a travel paste is read and charged, then refused with shareTarget.readerOff.
  • useDocumentToActivity emits no logEvent at all — only reportError. The busiest reader is the one that cannot be triaged from CloudWatch, against the observability rule in CLAUDE.md.
  • Consent order is inverted between doors. Pages ask before the picker; the FAB asks after, so declining there discards a photo already taken.
  • "Not recognised" behaves four ways. Activities open the form anyway, travel aborts, recipes abort with different copy, share shows a fourth message. And recipe-flavoured strings reach non-recipe captures, so a shared school newsletter can be told it has no transcript.

3The surface, and the moment it turns on

The field is the hero and is focused on open, so pasting costs nothing. Camera and file sit underneath, quieter. The row at the bottom is the new part: it shows what beanies can make without ever asking you to pick, then resolves to what it actually made.

Play the resolve

Three faint tiles at rest are a capability statement, not a question — and they carry no header, because the layout and the resolve already say what they are. They tick while beanies reads, then two fade and one lifts into the brand gradient with a single sheen pass.

The shimmer is one idiom at three speeds: slow and barely there on the field at rest, quick while reading, one pass on the tile that wins. It is the same 105° gradient the shipped MagicReaderCard already paints, so the magic reads as this feature's own language rather than a new effect. All of it stops under prefers-reduced-motion.

And it earns its keep: when inference lands wrong, not right? re-routes the same extraction to another kind. That is a question a parent can now answer correctly, because they can see what beanies made of it — and it costs no second read.

4Every state, both modes

Authored for light and dark in the same pass. Dark uses the semantic scale — ink on surface-raised, tiles on surface-overlay, Heritage Orange lifted to accent-lift #FF8B5E for the tagline, the re-route link and the sheen — accents get lighter on dark, never darker. 6Counting every magic bean, per family

One surface is what makes a trustworthy meter possible, but it is not the meter. Today there is no count that could carry entitlement: the only per-family number is a localStorage budget of 20 text reads an hour, which is per device and clearable, covers the bare-text arm only, and is invisible to the server. Three devices is three budgets.

The one rule that matters

The count of record is written server-side by the ai-extract Lambda, because that is the single chokepoint every read must pass through. A client-side count can never carry entitlement — it is per device, it is editable, and a modified client simply does not send it. The client keeps a count only to show a family where they stand.

  • Make familyId required, not optional. It is familyId? in ExtractOptions today, and useDocumentToActivity and useDocumentToTravel both omit it — so those two doors are already uncounted and reach only the IP limit. Required at the type level means a future door that forgets it fails the build instead of quietly leaking free reads.
  • Separate counting from limiting. The existing limiter is fail-open, which is right for a limiter — a DynamoDB blip should not lock a family out. It is wrong for a meter: a fail-open count silently loses reads, and usage built on it cannot be defended to a customer. A count that cannot be written gets recorded as a known gap and alerted on, never dropped.
  • One atomic increment per read, keyed by family and day. pk=family#<id>, sk=usage#<YYYY-MM-DD>, DynamoDB ADD. Day is the finest grain the pricing page sells, and week and month are then a range query rather than a second counter to keep in step.
  • Carry an attempt id so the write is idempotent. A retried invocation or a client retry over a dropped response must not double-count. This is cheap to add now and impossible to backfill later.
  • Read the usage back from day one. A write-only meter cannot be verified. Surfacing reads-per-family in /beanies-metrics makes it both a cost number and a proof that the meter works, well before entitlement depends on it.
  • Retire the localStorage budget once the meter lands. Two sources of truth that disagree is worse than one that is occasionally strict. It becomes a display of the server's number, or it goes.

This part needs your call, because the pricing page sells a number. "One magic bean" has to mean something a family can predict and we can defend. My proposal:

Case Counts? Why
A read the model answered 1 bean The billable unit. One model call, one bean.
A 12-page PDF 1 bean One call today. Page count is our cost problem, not the family's mental model.
Came back none — not recognised 1 bean The model ran and we paid for it. Counting it also stops "keep pasting until something sticks" being free.
not right? re-route free No second model call — the same extraction is handed to another reader.
Refused before the model: offline, consent declined, file too large, wrong type, reader off free Nothing was read. Note this means fixing the "billed then refused" defect above.
Our failure: 5xx, timeout, malformed response free Never bill a family for our outage. Counted separately as an error rate.
The recipe link ladder (video → transcript → extract) needs a decision It can make more than one upstream call for one user action. I would call it 1 bean and eat the variance, but it is a real cost asymmetry worth naming.

7What this changes, and what it deliberately does not

Changes
  • The three pinned doors open MagicBeansSheet instead of AiDocumentPicker. They keep their buttons — discoverability is the reason to have a magic-beans affordance on a page at all — but every button opens the same surface.
  • Text becomes an input everywhere, not just at the FAB. A pasted class-group message is the case with no other home today.
  • The detected kind decides where the result goes, so pasting a party invite while standing on Travel makes an activity. Landing somewhere you did not expect is why the resolve moment and not right? exist.

Deliberately not
  • No per-surface hint passed to the extractor. Biasing inference by the page you happened to be on makes the same input produce different results depending on where you stood — unreproducible in support, and it quietly reintroduces the "what IS this?" guess that shipping direction B removed. One input, one answer, everywhere.
  • No new camera plumbing. AiDocumentPicker stays exactly as it is and stays mounted, because the mixed image+PDF accept routes to the documents picker with no camera entry on native. It stops being a surface and goes back to being the file mechanism the sheet calls.
  • No fourth reader. This is a rollout of the existing three, not a new kind. MAGIC_READERS and the totality test are untouched.
