Magic beans: to-dos, and a shared result

Tracker #113, mockup of 2026-09-29. Magic beans learns a fifth kind, the to-do, and when one submission holds an activity and to-dos it returns both in one review box. Every surface below is drawn light and dark; use the toggle.

The example submission, a forwarded school email: "Year 3 field trip to the Science Centre on Tuesday 13 October, 8:30am to 2:30pm. Please return the signed permission slip by Friday 9 October. Children need sunscreen, a hat and a small bag with a packed lunch on the day. Trip fee $12 via the school portal: https://portal.example-school.org/trips"

Family: Greg (shared it), Willa (owns the School Forms card in Who Owns What), Leo 10, Max 7, Sam 5.

1. Processing overlay, shared answer

The reading overlay gains a fifth tile, To-do. When the answer holds both, two tiles lift together and the line under them says what was found, in place of "Counting magic beans...".

Five tiles do not fit one row on a phone, so the phone grid becomes three over two (today it is two by two for four). Both lit tiles use the same orange resolve; the second lift is the only new motion.

2. Review box, shared result

One box, two parts. The activity is a read-only summary: it is edited in the activity form, which opens straight after. The to-dos are edited here, one row each: title, who (with the reason it was picked), when, and a skip. The button says what happens next.

A. Right-side drawer (recommended)

The StatementImportModal shape. The activity form that follows is also a drawer (ActivityModal), so on desktop the next step opens in the same place and the hand-off reads as one flow.

5. Deleting an activity that has linked to-dos (stretch)

The existing ConfirmModal with its radio choice (confirmChoice). Default is keep the to-dos: a paid fee or a packed bag still matters if the trip is deleted by mistake or re-added, and a delete that quietly takes other people's tasks with it is the harder mistake to undo. Only open to-dos are listed; done ones are always kept.
