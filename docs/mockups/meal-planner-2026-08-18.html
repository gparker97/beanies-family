Meal Planner

The Treehouse 🌳 · Planning · the family meal board

A week-first meal board: days across the top, your cookbook down the side. Drag a recipe onto any day + slot — or drop an "eat out / leftovers / skip". The meal leads each card, with who's cooking clear beneath. Mark a meal cooked and it logs to the recipe. Clamped to the beanies CIG — Heritage Orange for prompts, squircle corners, Outfit / Inter / Caveat. A direction for review, not final UI.
