Tracker #116, revision 3 (greg, 2026-09-30). Ingredients are listed as written. Each recipe says how many times it gets cooked that week (Cook ×3), from who's eating against its servings, and its lines end in (×3) so the flat list still says to buy three lots. Identical lines merge on their own; ✨ Find Duplicates asks magic beans for the same item written differently.

1The Meal Planner header

"Shopping List" sits beside Share and Export as PDF and covers the week shown. The count is the number of recipes it will use (Tacos twice still counts once). With no recipe meals that week it is disabled, with a one-line reason under it.

2The week's list, by recipe

One section per recipe, in the order it's first served, with its lines as written. The green Cook ×3 marker says how many batches that week (Tue 5 eating + Fri 3, serves 4: 2 + 1). Nobody picked counts as everyone. Every line of a ×3 recipe ends in (×3), so the list in the Lists page still says to buy three lots. Lines that are exactly the same in two recipes are merged on their own into In More Than One Meal; ✨ Find Duplicates asks magic beans once for the rest.

The button follows the house magic beans style (Heritage Orange to Terracotta, white, ✨, the shimmer), shaped like the Quick Start card in the add drawers, at the top of the drawer because it's the one magic action here. One word of copy and a Free tag: it doesn't use your magic beans. It shows only when the week has 2+ recipes and AI is available, the same as the other magic beans buttons.

4The recipe page and the recipe form

From the recipe page there's no meal, so the Shopping List sheet (#88) uses the recipe's own amounts, now in the same checklist instead of the free-text box. The recipe form's Servings becomes a number ("Serves 4"). Old text is converted once by taking its first number ("Serves 4-6" becomes 4). Text with no count, like "12 muffins", is cleared.
