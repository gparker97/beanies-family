🛒 Shopping List from the Meal Planner

Tracker #116, revised after greg's review (direction B, simplified). The week's list is grouped by recipe. A recipe planned twice is one section. Amounts are scaled by how many are eating each time against the recipe's servings, rounded up to a whole batch per meal, then added up. If either number is missing, the recipe's own amounts are used. No merging across recipes and no duplicate checks.

1The Meal Planner header

"Shopping List" sits beside Share and Export as PDF and covers the week shown. The count is the number of recipes it will use (Tacos twice still counts once). With no recipe meals that week it is disabled, with a one-line reason under it.

2The week's list, by recipe

One section per recipe, in the order it's first served. The header says when it's served, how many are eating, and the batch count (×3). Lines with an amount are scaled (the old amount is shown faintly); lines without one ("salt to taste") are kept as written. Each section has its own Untick All, so "we already have everything for the stir-fry" is one tap. Every line can still be edited, and you can add your own.

4The recipe page and the recipe form

From the recipe page there's no meal, so the Shopping List sheet (#88) uses the recipe's own amounts, now in the same checklist instead of the free-text box. The recipe form's Servings becomes a number ("Serves 4"). Old text is converted once by taking its first number ("Serves 4-6" becomes 4). Text with no count, like "12 muffins", is cleared.
