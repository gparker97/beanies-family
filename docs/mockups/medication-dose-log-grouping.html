💊 Medication dose log — date grouping + over-limit warnings

Two changes. (A) The "Recent doses" list in the medication drawer is now grouped by day, each day showing how many doses were given and flagging days that went over the medication's recommended doses-per-day. (B) When logging a dose that would exceed the recommended daily count, the confirm dialog shows a gentle, dismissable, informative heads-up — never blocking, never alarming.

Example medication: Calpol (Paracetamol) — 5ml, every 4–6 hours, recommended 3 doses/day. The "Today" group below has 4 doses logged → 1 over the recommendation.
