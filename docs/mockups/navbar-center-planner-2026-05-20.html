Why this fits

The brand spec already reserves a dedicated 📅 planner/calendar slot (beanies-theme/SKILL.md) — this realigns the bar with documented intent. Heritage Orange + Outfit label are on-system.

What moves

Activities (/activities) graduates from the Planning popout to the center as Calendar. Planning popout keeps Travel + To-do. No routes change.

Even distribution

All five slots are flex: 1 — equal width. The round button only overlays the center slot, so spacing stays perfectly even left-to-right.

Edge cases

When Money hides (no finance access) the bar rebalances to four even slots with the hero still centered. The badge reuses the existing count model.
