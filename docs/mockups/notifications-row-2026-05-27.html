At-a-glance row

Emoji (kind) · bold title (the thing) · summary (who · where · a chip for your drop-off / pick-up duty) · the real date + time. Overdue tasks turn the time line Heritage Orange.

2

Read/unread on the row

The right-edge pip is the toggle: solid orange = unread (tap → read), hollow ring = read (tap → unread). Tapping the row body still opens the detail (and marks read). No need to open something just to change its read-state.

3

Detail = one action

Open is the sole primary button. "Mark as unread" is demoted to a faint text link beneath it (or dropped entirely, since the row's pip covers it) — it never competes with Open again.
