Give The Bean Pod its own place in the sidebar

On the phone, Pod is already a tab of its own, next to Planning and Money. On desktop it sits two levels down inside The Treehouse, the only nested group in the whole sidebar. This mockup lifts it out as a third section, The Bean Pod, anchored by the hugging beanies on every surface that leads to it.

Desktop would match the phone

The phone keeps its short, literal tab names; the Pod tab takes the same hugging beanies.

Choices in this mockup

Name
The Bean Pod on desktop, matching the name of the family's data file (the .beanpod): the pod is the family, and its file is the family's bean pod. The phone keeps the short Pod, as its other tabs are shorter too (Planning, Money).
Anchor
The hugging beanies, shrunk to icon size, wherever the app leads to The Bean Pod: the sidebar section, the phone's Pod tab, and any other entry point. It replaces the seedling 🌱.
Colour
Sky Silk for the section label, alongside Heritage Orange (Treehouse) and green (Piggy Bank), so each section has its own colour.
Order
Treehouse, Piggy Bank, Pod: the phone's order, from everyday planning to family reference.
