2. Read-only: a band, not a wall

Trial over, or plan lapsed. The Nook is still the Nook: every card, every number, every list is there and readable. The band states what changed and what still works. On the web it offers the plan page. On iOS and Android it is a statement of fact and an Export button, with no link, URL, price or purchase verb (Apple 3.1.3(f), Google Play payments policy).
