Recovery kits: see them, retire them

Today Settings says only “2 kit(s) on file”. Both directions below list every kit by the ID printed on it, keep invalidated kits visible so an old printout can be recognised as dead, and never let the last live kit go without a replacement. They differ only in where the list lives.

Direction A: the list lives in the Security & Recovery drawer

Mirrors the passkeys list in Account & Sign-In. The kits replace the bare count. Everything is one scroll away, no extra modal. Costs vertical space in a drawer that already holds the trusted-device toggle, magic link and Google cards.
