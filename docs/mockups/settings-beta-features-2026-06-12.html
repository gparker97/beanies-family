How it behaves: the “The Beanie Lab” row sits quietly below Quick toggles — collapsed, dashed outline, low-contrast, no orange, the beaker glyph still. Tap to expand → the beaker starts bubbling and the lightning flickers, with a warm caution + the Enable experimental features switch (off by default, per-device). Switch on → the Google Calendar and beanies AI cards slide in, each tagged Testing; tapping one opens its normal settings drawer. Switch off → they retract and AI/Calendar disappear from Settings entirely. The only persistent footprint for everyone else is one muted, collapsed row at the bottom of the page.
