What changes, in order of effect. (1) Only the section that owns the current route starts open; the other two fold to a header and a peek strip of their pages, so nothing is more than one tap away and the list fits a 740px viewport with room to spare. (2) Rows drop from 18px to 16px type, which is what stops Emergency Contacts and Family Milestones wrapping at 256px. (3) Help and Discord become two small buttons on the member card instead of two full rows. (4) When a person opens a second section on a short screen, the scrollbar is thin, hidden until hover, and the list fades at its edges. Opening a section still remembers your choice, as today.
