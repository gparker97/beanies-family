shared details, both directions

What every row carries

  • A friendly merchant name from the reader; the bank's raw text beneath it, kept on the saved transaction as "on your statement as".
  • Amount in Outfit: Heritage Orange going out, soft green coming in (CR lines, refunds).
  • A category chip that opens the family's category picker. Suggested from past entries for the same merchant.
  • Foreign-currency lines show the original amount as a Sky Silk chip; the SGD amount is what is saved.
  • A payment from another family account is suggested as a transfer, with the other account named.
  • Tap a row to edit date, amount, account or category inline; the tick decides whether it is added.

Defaults

  • Unmatched: ticked, add.
  • Familiar: merge.
  • Already imported (fingerprint match): unticked, skip.
