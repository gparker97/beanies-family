my read

one · warm wash

Strongest system fit — every special state gets a gradient. Pairs with someday's Sky Silk wash like two sides of one design language: warm vs. calm.

recommended

two · lane marker

Calmest. Reads as a hint not a state. Risk: the stripe might feel out of place since no other row in the system uses a side accent.

three · pill only

Lightest touch. Risk: a scanning user might miss it — the pill is small and the card looks identical to "due in three weeks".

four · outlined glow

Most attention-grabbing. Risk: gets close enough to overdue's intensity that the ladder flattens — today and overdue start to feel like a single "urgent" cluster.
