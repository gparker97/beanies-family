Who Owns What, round 8

A board that fills the screen, and a card you can hold

Two changes before the next deploy. The deal board gives its space to the people holding cards, and a bean with nothing yet folds into one strip you can still deal to. The card details drawer opens on the card itself, with arrows and swipe to move through the pile.

App frames:

1. The deal board (desktop)

A realistic family: greg holds 14 cards, Sofia 17, Leo 3, Mia none yet, and 9 are skipped. 39 still to deal.

What changes

  • The board fills the screen below the tabs. The rail's list and the lanes each scroll on their own, so the rail no longer sets the board's height.
  • Lane chips become small cards: the art at 42px, the name, and the split part or the done line. A lane reads at a glance.
  • A bean holding nothing folds into one strip at the top. Each face is still a drop target, and gets a full lane once it holds a card.
  • Skipped folds to one row with a Show link, and is still a drop target.

Why this way

  • The wall already folds idle beans into one strip, so the pattern is familiar.
  • Nothing that works today is lost: every member and Skipped can still take a dropped card.
  • Lanes keep the family's order and grow with their cards, so the space goes to whoever holds the most.

Your call

  • Idle strip at the top (shown) or at the bottom above Skipped?
  • Second line on a lane card: the done line (shown), or the category?
  • Skipped folded by default (shown), or open?

2. Card details, desktop

Opened from the Deck. The card sits under the title, exactly as it looks when it is dealt, with who holds it on the card. The arrows (and ← →) move through the list you opened it from.

3. Card details, phone

The same card at the deal pile's phone size. Swipe left or right on the card, or use the arrows. The details scroll underneath.

What changes

  • The card face is shared with the deal pile (one component), so the two never drift apart.
  • "Done looks like" moves onto the card; the drawer no longer repeats it below.
  • The header icon goes: the card right under it already shows the art.
  • Held by and since live on the card. Before that, split parts and "beanies uses this card for" stay below.

Moving between cards

  • Order is the list you opened it from: the Deck in its current filter, a board lane, or the Overview list.
  • Arrows beside the card on every width, ← → on a keyboard, swipe on touch.
  • "Home & Household · 1 of 15" above the card says where you are, like the deal pile does.

Your call

  • Drop the header icon (shown), or keep it?
  • On the phone: position dots under the card are only useful for short lists. Leave them out (the counter above does the job)?
