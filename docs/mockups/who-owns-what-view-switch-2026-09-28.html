A. Icon over label

The clearest: each tile reads as a picture of the layout with its name. Tallest of the three, so the row gains a little height.

B. One joined control

Reads as a single "view" control, like a desktop app toolbar. Same height as the buttons beside it, so the row stays slim.

C. Icons only

The most compact. The current view is named under the icons, and each icon names itself on hover. Relies most on the drawings reading at a glance.

Your call

Pick A, B or C. My lean is B: it is the most clearly "one control, two layouts" and sits level with Share the Deck.
